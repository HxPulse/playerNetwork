mo.co Player Network
This network includes all players featured in the global Top 200 of a Rift leaderboard.
It highlights players who share the most leaderboard placements with each other.

→ Connections: Number of unique players you share leaderboard placements with.
→ Matches: Number of shared leaderboard placements with a specific player.

Click a node to highlight that player in red and reveal their strongest connection (the player with the most shared placements).

You can adjust the parameters to generate different versions of the graph.

Lower values produce a larger graph (with more players), but also increases loading times.
